Dennis Shelton
Founder & Systems Administrator — It’s IT LLC
 
Dennis Shelton founded It’s IT LLC over 13 years ago on a straightforward premise: businesses deserve more than a computer guy. They deserve a full IT department. With 25+ years of hands-on experience spanning network engineering, infrastructure design, virtualization, and cybersecurity, Dennis brings enterprise-level expertise to organizations of every size throughout Southwest Florida.
 
His approach is direct, deeply technical, and built around results. He has a rare ability to take abstract infrastructure concepts and translate them into real-world systems that actually perform — then explain them clearly to the people who rely on them. Clients don’t just get IT support. They get a strategic technology partner who thinks about their business.
 
Before launching It’s IT LLC, Dennis built his expertise across diverse industries — from Sony technical support to network engineering roles at Softrim and VTech — giving him a uniquely broad perspective on what businesses actually need from their technology.
