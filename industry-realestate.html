Real Estate IT

Technology That Moves as Fast as the Market

Real estate is a mobile business where a missed email or inaccessible document can cost you a deal. Agents need instant access to MLS listings, transaction documents in DotLoop or Skyslope, CRM data in Follow Up Boss or kvCORE, and e-signatures through DocuSign — all from a phone at a showing or a laptop at a coffee shop. Your technology has to be seamless, fast, and available everywhere.

Real estate is also one of the top targets for wire fraud and Business Email Compromise (BEC) attacks. The FBI reports over $446 million lost to real estate wire fraud annually, with attackers impersonating agents, title companies, and lenders to redirect closing funds. A single compromised email account can cost your clients their entire down payment — and your brokerage its reputation.

Pain Points We Solve

IT Challenges Real Estate Professionals Face

Mobile Workforce Challenges

Agents who can't pull up a listing in Follow Up Boss, send a DocuSign at a showing, or access MLS data from the field lose deals to competitors who can. Your mobile workforce needs reliable, secure access to CRM, transaction management, and communication tools from any device — without VPN headaches or sync failures.

CRM & Platform Integration

Real estate professionals juggle 5–10 platforms daily — Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, or Salesforce for CRM; DotLoop or Skyslope for transactions; DocuSign for signatures; plus MLS, marketing automation, and accounting. When these tools don't talk to each other, agents waste hours on manual data entry and risk errors at closing.

Wire Fraud & Email Compromise

The FBI reports $446M+ lost to real estate wire fraud annually. Attackers monitor email chains, then send fake wiring instructions that redirect closing funds — often impersonating agents or title companies. A single compromised email account can cost your clients their entire down payment. Encrypted email and MFA are essential.

Wire Fraud Prevention Measures

  • Email encryption — Protect wiring instructions
  • MFA on all accounts — Block credential theft
  • DMARC/SPF/DKIM — Prevent email spoofing
  • BEC detection — Flag impersonation attempts
  • Staff training — Recognize social engineering
  • Verified callbacks — Confirm wire changes by phone
  • Dark web monitoring — Alert on leaked credentials
