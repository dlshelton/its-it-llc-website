Disaster Recovery & Backups

Automated backups, tested recovery plans, and business continuity — because downtime isn't an option.

Your Safety Net

When Disaster Strikes, You'll Be Ready

Southwest Florida knows what hurricanes can do — Ian proved that in 2022. But natural disasters are only one threat. Hardware failures, ransomware attacks, and human error can wipe out critical data just as fast. The question isn't whether something will go wrong — it's whether you'll be able to recover when it does.

IT's IT deploys Datto BCDR (Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery) for image-based backups with both on-site appliance and secure cloud replication. But we don't stop at backing up your data — we regularly test and verify that your backups actually restore. We work with every client to establish RPO (Recovery Point Objective) and RTO (Recovery Time Objective) targets so you know exactly how fast you'll be back up and running.

Pain Points We Solve

Why Businesses Lose Data

Backups That Were Never Tested

60% of backup restores fail because nobody verified them. A backup that doesn't restore isn't a backup — it's a false sense of security. IT's IT runs regular test restores so you know recovery works before you need it.

No Plan for Natural Disasters

SWFL hurricane season is real. Without off-site and cloud replication, a single storm can destroy your on-premise servers and every backup sitting next to them. You need geographic redundancy — data stored safely outside the blast radius.

Ransomware Recovery

Without clean, isolated backups, a ransomware attack means paying the ransom or losing everything. IT's IT maintains air-gapped backup copies that ransomware can't reach, giving you a way out without paying criminals.
