Pain Points We Solve

IT Challenges Retail & Hospitality Businesses Face

POS System Failures

A down register during peak hours is a direct hit to revenue and customer satisfaction. We provide proactive monitoring and rapid response to keep your point-of-sale systems running when you need them most.

PCI DSS Compliance

Any business that accepts credit cards must meet PCI DSS requirements. Non-compliance can result in fines, increased processing fees, and liability for card fraud. We help you get and stay compliant.

Customer WiFi Security

Offering guest WiFi is expected in hospitality — but improperly configured networks can expose your business systems to attack. We design segmented networks that keep guest and business traffic separate.
