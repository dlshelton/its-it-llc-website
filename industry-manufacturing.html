Pain Points We Solve

IT Challenges Manufacturers Face

Production Downtime

Unplanned IT failures on the production floor cost manufacturers $260,000+ per hour on average. A single switch failure or server crash can halt an entire production line. Proactive monitoring and predictive maintenance catches problems before they stop the line — and keeps your MES, ERP, and SCADA systems available around the clock.

OT/IT Security Gap

Manufacturing is the #1 targeted industry for ransomware, accounting for over 25% of all industrial attacks. As PLCs, HMIs, and IoT sensors connect to business networks, a single phishing email can reach production equipment. Without proper network segmentation, an office breach becomes a plant floor shutdown.

ERP & System Integration

Platforms like SAP, Epicor, NetSuite, and Fishbowl need to talk to your MES, warehouse management, and shipping systems seamlessly. Integration failures create inventory errors, double-entries, missed shipments, and costly manual workarounds that eat into your margins.
